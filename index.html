WHY MEDITATE?

Pause.
Pay attention.

Listen to a sound, notice your breathing or how your body feels. When your mind wanders, gently bring your attention back. Start with a few minutes.

You do not have to clear your mind or do it perfectly. Just give yourself a little time.

WHAT IS A PANIC ATTACK?

A sudden wave
of fear.

Your heart races, you tremble or feel short of breath. A panic attack can happen even when there is no clear danger.

Self does not replace medical care. If attacks recur or symptoms are new or severe, speak to a doctor.

SUPPORT DURING A PANIC ATTACK

Panic attack support.
One step at a time.

When it is hard to know what to do, follow one simple step at a time, at your own pace.

Explore panic attack support
01A rhythm for your breathing

Follow the animation or voice prompts to breathe in and out. If it feels uncomfortable, switch to grounding.

02Notice what is around you

Look at nearby objects, listen to sounds, feel your feet on the floor. Repeat or skip any step.

03After the attack

Note what happened and how long it lasted. Save a note and available heart-rate data. Look back later or export a PDF or CSV with the details you choose.
