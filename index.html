WHY MEDITATE?

Notice. Return.
Make it familiar.

Meditation can be a practice of paying attention: to a sound, your breathing or what you feel right now. When your mind wanders, you gently bring it back. You can begin with a short, comfortable session.

Repeating this gives you opportunities to notice tension, practise returning your attention and make time for yourself before a difficult day becomes overwhelming.

Research on mindfulness programmes suggests possible benefits for stress, anxiety and sleep. Results vary by person and type of practice; these findings describe programmes, rather than a clinical evaluation of Self.

UNDERSTANDING WHAT IS HAPPENING

When the body's alarm
feels too loud.

A panic attack is a sudden wave of intense fear or discomfort, sometimes with a racing heart, trembling or difficulty breathing. It can happen without an obvious danger or trigger.

There is no single explanation for every attack. Stress, inherited vulnerability and the body's fear response may play a role. An isolated attack does not by itself mean you have panic disorder.

If attacks recur or anxiety disrupts daily life, seek professional help. New or severe physical symptoms need medical assessment. Self offers tools for support and does not replace treatment.

SUPPORT DURING A PANIC ATTACK

When it feels too much.
One step at a time.

In a difficult moment, deciding what to do next can be hard. Self offers short, clear steps you can follow at your own pace.

01A rhythm for your breathing

A gentle animation guides an inhale and a longer exhale. You can look away and follow the voice instead. If focusing on breathing feels uncomfortable, move on to grounding.

02Something to focus on, here and now

Simple prompts turn your attention to nearby objects, sounds and the feeling of your feet on the ground. Try another prompt, repeat a step or skip it - you choose what fits.

03Space to reflect afterwards

Save the duration, circumstances, a personal note and available heart-rate data. Return to your history to notice patterns, or export it as PDF or CSV with the details you choose to include.

Voice guidance when you want it. Support on iPhone and Apple Watch.
